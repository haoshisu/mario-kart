# Original Kart Lab assets

`pulse-kart-v1.glb` and `pulse-wheel-v1.glb` are original project-generated
models, created with `scripts/build-models.py` using Blender 4.5.11 LTS.
Editable source scenes are kept in `art-source/`.

No third-party meshes, character designs, textures, fonts, or animation clips
were imported. These assets do not include Nintendo/Mario assets or branding.
