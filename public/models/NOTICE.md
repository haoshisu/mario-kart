# Original Kart Lab assets

`pulse-kart-v1.glb` and `pulse-wheel-v1.glb` are original project-generated
models, created with `scripts/build-models.py` using Blender 4.5.11 LTS.
`pulse-kart-v2.glb` and `pulse-wheel-v2.glb` are original Pulse R2 / Aster
assets generated by `scripts/build-racing-models.py` with the same Blender
version. They are retained as previous versions.

`kestrel-kart-v1.glb`, `kestrel-kart-low-v1.glb`, and `kestrel-wheel-v1.glb`
are retained as the first Kestrel / Aster version. They were generated by
`scripts/build-kestrel-models.py` from the user-approved original surface
study in `art-source/kestrel-study/`, made with the same Blender version.
Reference pictures informed quality and proportions; no reference images,
logos, characters or proprietary meshes are embedded in the assets.

`kestrel-kart-v2.glb` and `kestrel-kart-low-v2.glb` are the previous Aster body and
driver assets, using the same approved vehicle surfaces and wheel v1.
The revised original Aster face, fitted suit and helmet are generated by
`scripts/kestrel_character.py`, called from `scripts/build-kestrel-models.py`.
The face and eyes are original mesh surfaces; no photographs or character
textures are embedded.

Editable source scenes are kept in `art-source/`.

No third-party meshes, character designs, textures, fonts, or animation clips
were imported. These assets do not include Nintendo/Mario assets or branding.
Project ownership/licensing applies; this notice does not grant a separate
third-party or open-source license.


## Taiwan environment v1 / low v1 (2026-10-08)

Original architecture, cedar and harbor structures authored in this repository with
Blender. No downloaded models, photographs, Nintendo assets or third-party texture
assets are included. Source: `scripts/build-taiwan-environment.py`; editable exports:
`art-source/taiwan-environment-v1.blend` and
`art-source/taiwan-environment-low-v1.blend`. Runtime files:
`public/models/taiwan-environment-v1.glb` and
`public/models/taiwan-environment-low-v1.glb`.

Taiwanese landmarks provide architectural inspiration. The gate, shop houses, tea
houses, wooden stations, segmented tower and harbor structures are original
fictional designs with altered proportions and arrangement. Shared eight-material
vertex colors and metre-scale UVs use repository-generated surface textures.
`doc/images/track-redesign/taipei-concept-v1.png` is an AI concept and is not a
texture, mesh or game screenshot.

## Chibi crew v1 (2026-10-15)

`chibi-{dodo,lumi,lili,bibo}-v1.glb` and their `-low-v1.glb` counterparts
are the current playable original characters on the approved Kestrel vehicle.
Generated by `scripts/build-chibi-karts.py` using the approved Dodo chibi
study and original Lumi, Lili and Bibo geometry; Blender sources and uncompressed
exports are in `art-source/chibi-karts/`. The existing Kestrel wheel v1 is reused.
The four portraits in `public/characters/` are Blender renders of these models,
produced by `scripts/render-chibi-karts.py`. No external character assets are used.

## Taiwan city street dressing v1 (2026-10-17)

`taiwan-city-v1.glb` and `taiwan-city-low-v1.glb` contain original storefronts,
awnings, balconies, rooftop tanks, tea furniture, lanterns and harbor shops.
Source: `scripts/build-taiwan-city.py`; editable files and regeneration notes:
`art-source/taiwan-city/`. These supplement the existing environment modules.
Shop names and arrangements are fictional. Letter geometry was generated using
the macOS STHeiti Light system font; no font file is included or downloaded at
runtime. No third-party models, photographs or textures are used in this kit.
